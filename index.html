Community rules

Protect people, not one approved interpretation

These boundaries apply to every story and response. They make room for uncertainty while keeping the community humane.

01

Respect the person

Disagree with ideas without degrading, mocking or harassing people.

02

Offer possibilities

Do not diagnose, declare certainty, or issue divine commands.

03

Protect privacy

Do not expose names or identifiable details about third parties.

04

Do not exploit

No solicitation, pressure, dangerous advice or financial approaches.

05

Follow the response choice

Listen when the storyteller asks only to be heard.

06

Be genuine and lawful

No impersonation, spam, threats or fabricated evidence.

Important: Published stories would be reviewed for privacy, safety and these rules—not independently verified as fact.

Why this exists

The kind of place the founder once needed

Unusual experiences and spiritual questions have been part of my life for many years. I am writing a book about the experiences that shaped that path, but I do not claim final answers or professional authority.

Project Supernatural is honest about the difference between what happened and what we think it may mean.

Separate and voluntary

Support never changes your place here

The community remains free. Financial support never affects access, publication, moderation, visibility or how anyone is treated.
