About the project

Built from experience, without claiming final answers

Unusual experiences and spiritual questions have been part of the founder’s life for many years. Project Supernatural is being shaped as the kind of careful, open community that was once difficult to find.

The founder is not presented as a spiritual authority, therapist or researcher. The project keeps a clear distinction between what someone experienced and what any of us think it may mean.

Financial support never affects access, publication, moderation or how anyone is treated.

Community rules

Protect people. Leave conclusions open.

  • Respect the person, even when questioning an interpretation.
  • Do not diagnose, threaten, exploit or issue divine commands.
  • Protect the privacy of everyone named in a story.
  • Follow the storyteller’s selected response preference.

Prototype status

This is a static design prototype. There are no accounts, public submissions, live comments, analytics or tracking. Example stories are fictional interface samples and are not presented as real experiences.
