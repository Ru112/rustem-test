How it will work

The writer chooses the response

Not every experience needs analysis or debate. A future published story would carry one of these preferences.

  • Just listen

    Acknowledge the experience without analysis.

  • Similar experiences

    Share something comparable that happened to you.

  • Different perspectives

    Offer sources or possibilities without forcing a conclusion.

  • Open discussion

    Discuss possibilities while respecting the person.

The project

Experience first. Interpretation second.

People can be certain that something affected them while remaining uncertain about its cause. Project Supernatural is being designed around that distinction.

A future archive would let people read accounts by subject and response preference. Publication would mean a story passed privacy and conduct review, not that the event was verified as fact.

Community principles

Clear boundaries for a future community

  1. 01

    The writer chooses the response.

    Discussion follows the preference attached to the story.

  2. 02

    Different explanations can coexist.

    Belief, doubt and uncertainty are all allowed.

  3. 03

    Disagreement stays focused on ideas.

    Ridicule, diagnosis, threats and spiritual commands are not allowed.

  4. 04

    Moderation checks privacy and conduct.

    It does not decide what is metaphysically true.
