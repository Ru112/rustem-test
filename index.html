Choose how you want people to respond

Not every story needs advice or debate. The person sharing decides what kind of response would be helpful.

  • Just listen

    No analysis or debate. Acknowledge the experience respectfully.

  • Similar experiences

    Share something comparable that happened to you.

  • Different perspectives and sources

    Offer spiritual, religious, historical or grounded perspectives without forcing a conclusion.

  • Open respectful discussion

    Discuss different possibilities while respecting the person and the experience.

Why I started this project

I started Project Supernatural because I know what it is like to carry an experience that is difficult to explain. I do not have final answers, and I am not building a place that tells people what they must believe. I am building a place where people can describe what happened, protect their privacy and hear different perspectives without being mocked.

Support the project ↗

Support is optional. It never affects access, publication, moderation or how anyone is treated.

Community rules

  • Question an interpretation without attacking the person.
  • Do not ridicule, diagnose or give spiritual commands.
  • Protect the privacy of everyone mentioned in a story.
  • Follow the storyteller’s response preference.

Prototype status

This is a static prototype. Accounts, public submissions and live comments are not open. It has no analytics or tracking. Every example story is fictional and is not presented as a real experience.
