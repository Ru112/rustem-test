How would you like people to respond?

When a story is eventually published, this choice tells readers what kind of replies the storyteller welcomes.

  • Acknowledge onlyLet the person know they were heard. No analysis or debate.
  • Share a similar experienceReply with something genuinely comparable.
  • Offer perspectives and sourcesSuggest possibilities or useful sources without declaring a final answer.
  • Open respectful discussionDiscuss different interpretations while respecting the storyteller.

How it will work

A story comes first. The conversation follows the storyteller.

  1. 01
    Describe what happened

    Start with your own account. You do not need to settle what it means.

  2. 02
    Set the kind of reply

    Choose whether readers acknowledge, compare, offer sources or discuss.

  3. 03
    Keep the choice

    Later, decide whether the draft should remain private or be considered for publishing.

About the founder

Why I am building Project Supernatural

Unusual experiences have been part of my life for as long as I can remember. Some reached me through events and stories in my family. Others left me with questions that stayed with me for years.

When I looked for places to speak about these experiences, I often found one of two extremes: people who dismissed the experience before hearing it, or people who were certain they already knew exactly what it meant. Neither felt honest to me.

I began writing a book about the events that shaped this path. While working on it, I realized that the book should not be the only place where experiences like these can be heard. Many people carry a dream, coincidence, vision, presence, prayer experience or moment they cannot easily explain. They may not want a verdict. They may simply want to put it into words, protect their privacy and hear thoughtful perspectives.

Project Supernatural is my attempt to build that place. I am not presenting myself as a spiritual authority, therapist or researcher. I am building it as someone who understands what it is like to carry questions that do not fit into a simple answer.

Project Supernatural is an independent project still in development. Optional support helps pay for hosting, development, moderation tools, translation and the future publication of the book that inspired the project.

Support Project Supernatural

Support is voluntary and never affects access, publication or moderation. Project Supernatural is not a registered charity, and support is not tax-deductible.

Payments are processed by PayPal. PayPal may provide limited transaction information such as your name, email address and payment amount. This information is not displayed publicly or connected to a community nickname.

Community principles

People come before conclusions.

  • The storyteller sets the response.Readers will be asked to follow the response preference on each post.
  • Disagree with ideas, not people.Ridicule, diagnosis, threats and spiritual commands are not allowed.
  • Interpretation stays separate.Moderation protects privacy and conduct; it does not decide what is metaphysically true.
