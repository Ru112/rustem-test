Why I’m building this

For questions that do not fit a simple answer.

Unusual experiences have been part of my life for as long as I can remember. Some reached me through events and stories in my family. Others left me with questions that stayed with me for years.

When I looked for places to speak about these experiences, I often found one of two extremes: people who dismissed the experience before hearing it, or people who were certain they already knew exactly what it meant. Neither felt honest to me.

I’m building this as someone who understands what it is like to carry questions that do not fit into a simple answer.

Community principles

What the project is trying to protect.

  • Accounts, not claimsAn experience does not have to be defended.
  • The storyteller sets the termsReply preferences are respected.
  • Doubt is allowedFor readers and storytellers alike.
  • Privacy and conduct matterModeration does not decide metaphysical truth.
