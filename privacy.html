Privacy

What this prototype stores

The current site has no accounts, analytics, tracking or public submissions.

Private drafts

A draft is stored through local browser storage on the device where it is written. The prototype does not send, publish or upload that draft. Clearing browser data can remove it.

No promise of anonymity

The future service may let a storyteller use a nickname publicly. A nickname does not guarantee complete anonymity. Details inside a story can still identify someone.

External services

The Support button opens PayPal in a new tab. PayPal applies its own terms and privacy policy when someone visits that service.

This draft is not a final legal policy. It must be reviewed before accounts or public submissions open.
