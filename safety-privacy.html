Safety & Privacy

Write carefully before sharing publicly

A nickname can limit what appears publicly. It cannot remove identifying details from a story.

Before writing

  • Leave out full names, addresses, workplaces and contact details.
  • Do not include another person’s medical records or private messages.
  • Consider whether a date, place or unusual event could identify someone.
  • Keep a separate copy if the draft matters to you. Browser storage can be cleared.

When discussion opens in the future

Moderation is intended to address privacy and conduct. It cannot promise that every harmful comment will be prevented or removed immediately.

Urgent situations

This project is not an emergency or professional-support service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
