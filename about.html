About

Why I started this project

A place for describing unusual experiences without requiring one explanation.

The reason for it

I started Project Supernatural because I know what it is like to carry an experience that is difficult to explain. I do not have final answers, and I am not building a place that tells people what they must believe.

I want people to be able to describe what happened, protect their public identity and hear different perspectives without being mocked.

What it is not

The founder is not presented as a therapist, researcher, spiritual guide or authority on supernatural events. Moderation is intended to protect privacy and conduct. It does not decide which interpretation is true.

Current status

This is a static prototype. Accounts, publishing and comments are not open. Private drafts remain in the browser on the device where they were written.
